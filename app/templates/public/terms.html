4. Unit Reservations

4.1 Reservation Process

To reserve a unit, you must:

  • Create an account and verify your email
  • Browse available units and select one
  • Submit your reservation with required information
  • Upload a valid ID of the tenants who will stay
  • Wait for admin review and approval

4.2 Required Documents

For contract processing, we require a valid ID of the tenants who will stay. Please upload clear, readable ID images to avoid delays. Our staff may request additional documents depending on your application.

4.3 Reservation Status

Your reservation status will be updated in the system. Possible statuses include:

  • Pending: Under review by our staff
  • Approved: Reservation accepted, proceed with contract
  • Rejected: Reservation not approved
  • Cancelled: Reservation cancelled by you or admin

4.4 Auto-Cancellation

If required follow-ups are not completed within the time window given by our team (typically 48 hours), the reservation may be automatically cancelled. This keeps the process fair for everyone waiting in line.

6.1 Payment Methods

6.2 Security Deposit

A security deposit equal to one month's rent plus ₱1,000 is required for all reservations (residential or commercial). For example, a unit with ₱4,500 monthly rent has a ₱5,500 deposit. This deposit is refundable after the contract ends, minus any damages and unpaid bills.

6.3 Monthly Rent

Monthly rent must be paid on or before the due date specified in your contract. Late payments may incur penalties as outlined in your lease agreement.
