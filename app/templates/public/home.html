About

The Elis Center Commercial and Residences is positioned along the highway with CCTV coverage for safety and security. The site is close to key landmarks including LPU Laguna, NU Laguna, WalterMart, and SM, making it practical for businesses and residents who value accessibility and visibility.

The Smart Tenant Management System (STMS) is our online way to browse units, reserve, and manage your tenancy documents in one place—so you spend less time on follow-ups and more time on your business or home life.

Whether you need a commercial space or a residential unit, our team guides you through viewing, requirements, and next steps. For the latest availability and rates, create an account and sign in—our staff will assist you through the process.

Residential units are all studio type. Commercial units are available under contract terms (minimum 1 year). Power and water bills are exclusive (paid separately). Payment methods: cash and bank transfer. For contract processing, we require a valid ID of the tenants who will stay.

FAQ

What is STMS and how does it help me?
STMS (Smart Tenant Management System) is the online portal of The Elis Center for browsing sample units, creating an account, and submitting a reservation. It helps you complete requirements digitally and follow updates faster than back-and-forth messages alone. After you sign in, you can see more details about availability and next steps with our team.
How do reservations work from start to finish?
Create an account (with email verification), sign in, then choose an available unit and submit your information. You will typically provide contact details, move-in date, and a valid ID. Your request is reviewed by our staff. If approved, you will be guided on the contract and move-in requirements. If you need changes, contact us using the official numbers on this website.
What documents or information do I need to reserve?
For the contract, we require a valid ID of the tenants who will stay. Please upload clear, readable ID images to avoid delays. Our staff will advise if additional documents are needed depending on your application.
Can I visit or inspect the property before deciding?
Yes—many applicants want to see the location and unit layout first. Call or message us during office hours (see Contact) so we can schedule a viewing that fits your schedule. For safety and building security, we recommend setting an appointment instead of walking in unannounced.
What happens if I do not complete requirements on time?
If a reservation cannot move forward or required follow-ups are not completed within the time window given by our team, the unit may be released to other applicants. This keeps the process fair for everyone waiting in line. If you have an emergency, contact us as early as possible so we can advise you.
How do I pay? What methods are accepted?
We accept cash and bank transfer. Power and water bills are exclusive (paid separately). After your application progresses, our office staff will provide official instructions (where to pay, deadlines, and official receipts). Please avoid sending money to personal accounts—only follow guidance from verified Elis Center contacts.
Is the building safe? Is there CCTV?
The property promotes safety with CCTV coverage in common areas. For your own security, always lock your unit, avoid sharing access codes, and report unusual incidents to the management office during business hours.
How long does it take to get a response after I submit?
Response time depends on office hours and the completeness of your documents. If your files are clear and your contact details are correct, our team can usually follow up sooner. If you do not hear back within a reasonable time, please call the numbers listed in Contact.
Is my personal information safe?
We use account verification and secure practices to protect your information. Keep your password private, and only use official contact numbers listed on this site. If someone asks for payments or personal details outside official channels, please verify with us first.
How can I reach the property team?
Use the phone numbers listed in the Contact section: 0918-912-6340 and 0918-920-5645. Hours are typically Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM. For urgent on-site concerns after you become a tenant, please follow the instructions provided in your tenancy process.
