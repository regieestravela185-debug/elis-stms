Privacy Policy

The Elis Center Commercial and Residences

Smart Tenant Management System (STMS)

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

1. Introduction

The Elis Center Commercial and Residences ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Smart Tenant Management System (STMS).

By using STMS, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you register for an account or use our services, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name: To identify you in our system
  • Email Address: For account verification and communication
  • Phone Number: For contact and emergency purposes
  • Valid ID: For identity verification and contract processing
  • Move-in/Move-out Dates: For reservation and contract management
  • Payment Information: For rent and deposit tracking

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you access STMS, we may automatically collect:

  • IP Address: For security and analytics
  • Browser Type and Version: For compatibility
  • Device Information: For responsive design
  • Login History: For security monitoring
  • Activity Logs: For system improvement

2.3 CCTV Footage

Our property has CCTV coverage in common areas for safety and security. By entering the premises, you consent to being recorded. CCTV footage is stored securely and accessed only when necessary for security purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and manage your account
  • Email Verification: To verify your email address through OTP
  • Reservation Processing: To process your unit reservations
  • Contract Management: To prepare and manage lease agreements
  • Payment Processing: To track rent and deposit payments
  • Communication: To send important updates and announcements
  • Concerns Management: To address your concerns and complaints
  • Security: To protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • System Improvement: To improve our services and user experience

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Internal Sharing

Your information is shared internally with:

  • Admin Staff: To process reservations and manage tenancies
  • Management: For oversight and decision-making
  • Maintenance Team: To address concerns and repairs

4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

  • Email Services: For sending OTP and notifications (Gmail SMTP)
  • Payment Processing: For bank transfers (if applicable)
  • Cloud Storage: For secure data backup

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, government agencies).

🔒 We Never Sell Your Information: We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Password Hashing: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • Secure Connections: HTTPS encryption for data transmission
  • Access Controls: Role-based access to sensitive information
  • Regular Backups: To prevent data loss
  • Security Monitoring: To detect and prevent unauthorized access

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

After your tenancy ends, we may retain certain information for record-keeping and legal compliance purposes. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Objection: Object to processing of your information
  • Portability: Request transfer of your information to another service
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 12.

8. Cookies and Tracking

STMS uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain your login session
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze site usage and performance
  • Improve user experience

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of STMS.

9. Email Communications

We may send you emails for:

  • Account Verification: OTP codes for email verification
  • Password Reset: Links to reset your password
  • Reservation Updates: Status changes on your reservations
  • Payment Reminders: Upcoming rent due dates
  • Announcements: Important updates from management
  • Concern Updates: Responses to your concerns

You can manage your email preferences in your account settings. However, you cannot opt out of essential service emails (e.g., OTP, password reset).

10. Children's Privacy

STMS is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on STMS. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through the system.

Your continued use of STMS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

  • Hours: Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

  • Facebook: ELIS Business Center | Calamba

13. Data Protection Officer

For data protection inquiries, you may contact our management team during office hours. We are committed to addressing your privacy concerns promptly and professionally.

14. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules and regulations.

By using STMS, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy.
